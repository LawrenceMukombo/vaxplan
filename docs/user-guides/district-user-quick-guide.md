# District Manager Quick Guide

## 1. Role Purpose
You oversee all health facilities within your district. Your job is to approve microplans, monitor stockouts, and ensure Hard-to-Reach (HTR) communities are covered.

## 2. Daily Tasks
- Review and approve submitted microplans from facilities.
- Monitor the district dashboard for zero-dose clusters.
- Schedule and conduct supportive supervision visits.

## 3. Key Modules Used
- **Approvals:** To sign off on facility plans.
- **Supervision:** To score facility compliance.
- **Dashboard:** To view district-level coverage aggregation.

## 4. Step-by-step Workflow: Approving a Microplan
1. Navigate to **Approvals** in the sidebar.
2. Click on a facility's "Pending" microplan.
3. Review the planned outreach sessions vs. the assigned population.
4. If acceptable, click **Approve**. If not, click **Request Changes** and leave a note.

## 5. Common Mistakes
> [!WARNING]
> Do not ignore unassigned settlements. If a settlement is >50km from a facility, it is the district's responsibility to dispatch a mobile team. Check the **Hard-to-Reach** module weekly.

## 6. Troubleshooting Tips
> **Baby steps:** If you can't see a facility's data, check your filter settings at the top of the page.
- **Can't approve:** Ensure you have the 'District Manager' role assigned by the National Admin.

## 7. Escalation Path
Contact the **Provincial Coordinator** for stock reallocations or the **National Admin** for role changes.
