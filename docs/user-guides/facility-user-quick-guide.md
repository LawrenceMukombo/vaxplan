# Facility User Quick Guide

## 1. Role Purpose
As a Facility User, you are the backbone of the immunization system. Your job is to plan where and when vaccination sessions will happen, and record the results when children are vaccinated.

## 2. Daily Tasks
- Enter vaccination totals into the Sessions module after returning from the field.
- Check the 'Defaulter List' to find children who missed their follow-up doses.
- Draw or adjust the boundaries of the villages your facility serves.

## 3. Key Modules Used
- **Sessions:** To mark planned sessions as "Achieved" and enter data.
- **Facilities (Communities Tab):** To map your catchment area.
- **Microplan Wizard:** To build the quarterly plan.

## 4. Step-by-step Workflow: Recording a Session
1. Navigate to **Sessions** in the sidebar.
2. Find today's session on the calendar and click it.
3. Click **Mark session done**.
4. Enter the tally of antigens administered (e.g., Penta1: 15, OPV: 15).
5. Click **Submit**.

## 5. Common Mistakes
> [!WARNING]
> Do not wait weeks to sync your device. If you work offline, ensure you click the **Sync** button in the header as soon as you have an internet connection to prevent data loss.

## 6. Troubleshooting Tips
> **Baby steps:** If the map isn't loading your village, check your connection. 
- **Polygon won't save:** Ensure the lines of the shape you drew don't cross each other.
- **Can't submit Microplan:** Ensure you are logged in as an 'In-Charge'. Clerks can only save drafts.

## 7. Escalation Path
If you are locked out or cannot find a village in the registry, contact your **District Manager**.
