## Slide 2: The Global Challenge — The Last-Mile Immunization Gap

- **Slide Title**: The Problem: Fragmentation at the Health Facility Frontier
- **Subtitle**: Outdated, paper-based microplanning leaves millions unreached and billions wasted
- **Three Core Friction Points (Card Layout)**:
  1. **14.3 Million Zero-Dose Children**:
     - Remote, nomadic, conflict-affected, and urban-slum communities are completely invisible on static paper maps.
     - Catchment boundaries drawn by hand lack validated GPS coordinates and realistic travel-time estimates.
  2. **The Paper Microplanning Bottleneck**:
     - Health facilities spend 6–10 weeks manually compiling fragmented paper worksheets and static spreadsheets.
     - Data is error-prone, lost in transit, and impossible for district managers to aggregate in time for funding cycles.
  3. **Cold Chain & Supply Chain Mismatches**:
     - 20–30% of vaccines are wasted due to inaccurate buffer estimations, cold chain breakdowns, and stockouts.
     - Outreach teams arrive in villages without adequate ice packs, vaccine carriers, or syringes.
- **Key Metric Highlight Box**:
  - **$1.5 Billion+**: Annual global economic loss caused by vaccine-preventable disease outbreaks stemming from sub-optimal coverage.

> **Presenter Speaker Notes**:
> *"Despite monumental global progress, 14.3 million children received zero doses of life-saving vaccines last year. When we look into why this occurs, the root cause is rarely vaccine manufacturing — it is the breakdown of microplanning at the health facility level. Today, thousands of clinic nurses are forced to fill out 40-page paper templates by hand, guess population figures, and send paper files up to the district. By the time budgets are calculated, the operational window has passed, stockouts occur, and outreach sessions are cancelled. VaxPlan was built to eliminate this bottleneck forever."*

## Slide 5: Core Innovations & Breakthrough Capabilities

- **Slide Title**: Technological Innovations Driving Last-Mile Reach
- **Subtitle**: Modern, robust, and purpose-built for low-connectivity environments
- **Four Core Innovation Pillars (Grid Layout)**:
  1. **Offline-First PWA Architecture**:
     - Operates reliably in zero-connectivity rural clinics without internet access.
     - Local SQLite / IndexedDB sync engine with intelligent multi-master conflict resolution when connectivity resumes.
  2. **Precision Geospatial Analytics (PostGIS & MapLibre)**:
     - Catchment buffer analysis, terrain friction modeling, and travel-distance calculations.
     - Multi-layer spatial visualization (settlements, health facilities, roads, rivers, cold chain points).

     - Calculates precise doses, wastage factors, AD syringes, dilution syringes, and safety boxes across 10+ antigens.
     - Evaluates facility refrigerator net storage volume (litres) against forecasted shipment volumes to prevent spoilage.
  4. **Transparent Governance & Multi-Tier Approvals**:
     - Policy-governed workflow: 7-day mandatory stakeholder review gate prior to sign-off.
     - Configurable lead times between plan approval and session execution to safeguard logistics.

> **Presenter Speaker Notes**:
> *"Four key technical capabilities set VaxPlan apart from generic health software. First, our offline-first architecture guarantees that nurses in remote clinics without cell signal can complete plans without interruption. Second, our GIS engine combines satellite basemaps with open spatial data to pinpoint unreached settlements. Third, our cold chain calculator prevents catastrophic vaccine wastage by ensuring facility refrigerators have the physical net capacity to store arriving shipments. ## Slide 6: Enterprise Technology Stack & Interoperability

- **Slide Title**: Enterprise Architecture: Built for Scale, Security & Sovereignty
- **Subtitle**: Open standards, sovereign data ownership, and seamless health system interoperability
- **Three-Tier Architecture Diagram**:
  - **Client Tier**: React, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS, ShadCN UI, Lucide Icons, Leaflet / MapLibre GL.
  - **Application Tier**: Node.js, Express, RESTful APIs, Session Auth / RBAC Middleware, Automated Validation Pipelines.
  - **Data Tier**: PostgreSQL 16 + PostGIS Spatial Engine, Drizzle ORM, Automated Backups, Read Replicas.
- **Interoperability & Standards Compliance**:
  - **OpenHIE Aligned**: Ready for health information exchange integration.
  - **DHIS2 Direct Sync**: Bi-directional data pipeline for national aggregate immunization statistics.
  - **eLMIS Integration**: Interoperable with national electronic logistics management systems for vaccine order fulfillment.
  - **Data Sovereignty**: Deployable on national government clouds or local Tier-3 sovereign data centres.

> **Presenter Speaker Notes**:
> *"VaxPlan is built on a battle-tested, enterprise-grade open stack: PostgreSQL with PostGIS, Node.js, and React TypeScript. We adhere strictly to OpenHIE architectural principles, meaning VaxPlan does not create another data silo. It feeds directly into national DHIS2 instances and integrates with electronic Logistics Management Information Systems (eLMIS). Most importantly, we respect national data sovereignty: VaxPlan can be hosted within national government data centres with end-to-end encryption, ensuring health ministries retain 100% control over their population data."*

## Slide 9: Enterprise Multi-Tenant Governance & Security

- **Slide Title**: Rigorous Governance, Security & Multi-Tier Access
- **Subtitle**: Protecting health records while empowering stakeholders with role-specific visibility
- **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) Matrix**:
  - **National / MOH Superadmin**: National policy parameters, tenant creation, cross-province analytics, and national exports.
  - **Provincial / Regional Coordinator**: Provincial progress monitoring, inter-district resource balancing, and regional approvals.
  - **District Health Executive (DMO/EPI)**: Plan review, budget authorization, compliance validation, and field supervision tracking.
  - **Health Facility Planner (Nurse/In-Charge)**: Draft creation, settlement editing, session scheduling, and local workplan execution.
  - **Auditor / Donor / Partner**: Read-only oversight, expenditure tracking, coverage verification, and impact reporting.
- **Security & Reliability Highlights**:
  - Encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3).
  - Immutable version history capturing every draft open, edit, save, and approval.
  - Zero destructive database operations (Upsert-only architecture preserving historical microplans).

> **Presenter Speaker Notes**:
> *"Data security and transparent governance are paramount. VaxPlan incorporates strict Role-Based Access Control that mirrors the national health system hierarchy. Health facility staff can only edit their catchment plans, district managers review and approve, while national leaders and donors gain birds-eye analytical dashboards. Every single modification to a microplan generates an immutable version record, providing an airtight audit trail. Donors and auditors can inspect exactly when a plan was submitted, who approved the budget, and how every session was executed."*
